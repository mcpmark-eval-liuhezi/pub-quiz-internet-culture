## Q1 — Finish the lyric (main question)

**Question:**
Rick Astley promises a lot in the chorus of "Never Gonna Give You Up" — the song at the end of every rickroll. We give you the first two lines of the chorus; finish the next four.

> Never gonna give you up
> Never gonna let you down
> ...

**Answer key (chorus, quoted exactly as the official video transcript renders it):**

```
♪ Never gonna give you up ♪
♪ Never gonna let you down ♪
♪ Never gonna run around
and desert you ♪
♪ Never gonna make you cry ♪
♪ Never gonna say goodbye ♪
♪ Never gonna tell a lie
and hurt you ♪
```

*Source: official video transcript, "Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video) (4K Remaster)" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ — captured the same day as filing.*

Note for the host: the transcript renders lines 3 and 6 as two lines each ("Never gonna run around / and desert you", "Never gonna tell a lie / and hurt you"), with no comma after "around". Accept the full six lines; the ♪ marks are the transcript's, not part of the lyrics.

## Q2 — Release year

**Question:**
"Never Gonna Give You Up" wasn't born as a meme — it was a chart-topping single first. What year was it originally released as a single?

**Answer:** 1987

**Fact + source:** Per the song's Wikipedia infobox, the single was released **27 July 1987** (recorded October 1986), issued on RCA / PWL / BMG, written and produced by Stock Aitken Waterman, from the album *Whenever You Need Somebody*.

*Source: "Never Gonna Give You Up" — Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Never_Gonna_Give_You_Up — infobox "Released" row, checked the same day as filing.*

## Q3 — AI-history tiebreaker

**Question:**
The rickroll became a meme through forums and bots; modern bots run on models. Google's original BERT base model — the one trained on BookCorpus and English Wikipedia — is hosted on Hugging Face. Name the exact repository id, and for the tiebreak: what license does the listing show?

**Answer:**
- Repository id: `google-bert/bert-base-uncased`
- License: `apache-2.0`

*Source: Hugging Face model listing — https://huggingface.co/google-bert/bert-base-uncased — license shown on the listing, checked the same day as filing. (110.1M parameters, fill-mask task, datasets: bookcorpus + wikipedia.)*
